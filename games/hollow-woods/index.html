v1.2 patch notes: story mode, 10 worlds, 75 levels

Worlds

1 · The WoodsGrey forest, giant dead trees on the skyline, grass tufts.
2 · The MinesBlue-black caverns, stalactites, headframes with lanterns, rubble floors, long crawl tunnels.
3 · The MarshGreen bog, huts on stilts, reeds and cattails, crumbling planks and lever bridges.
4 · The FoundryRust-orange works, smoking chimneys, bolted floors, saw blades. Levels 4 and 8 are chases.
5 · The CitadelPale stone, glowing spires, battlements, every hazard mixed. Chases from level 4 on.

New in v1.1: five more worlds (5 levels each)

6 · The ReefSunken teal forest: light shafts, bubbles, floaty swimming jumps, kelp, jellyfish and crumbling coral.
7 · The DunesAmber desert: pyramids, cacti, blowing sand and a steady headwind.
8 · The GlacierIce everywhere: you slide, snow falls, spires of ice. Hot work for cold feet.
9 · The CindersVolcano country: lava glow, rising embers, traps running 20% hot.
10 · The VoidFloating monoliths and a haloed black sun. Fireflies, little light, and the last levels are chases.

Pits and other drops now glow with a pulsing white abyss, the chasing shadow has a glowing edge, and the boy now cycles through every idle animation (tap, scratch, fidget, look back, hug when cold, sit) while you stand still.

Every level number has its own look and rule (in every world)

1 · DawnWarm wash, calm. Opens with hills.
2 · GaleA headwind slows you while you move or jump. Blowing leaves. Crumbling planks.
3 · BlackoutYour light shrinks to a small circle. Fireflies drift. Crawl tunnel.
4 · FrostIcy ground: you slide and need room to stop. Snow falls throughout. Spike rows.
5 · DriftLow gravity for long, floaty jumps. Spores rise. Spring pads.
6 · DownpourRain throughout. Heavy gravity: jumps are fast and short. Wide pits.
7 · EmbersSpike traps and saws run 30% faster. Embers rise.
8 · HushFog throughout and less light, but traps run slower. Trap spikes.
9 · GloamViolet dusk. You run 20% faster, and the level is longer.
10 · HollowSmoke throughout. The shadow chases you.

Story, items and hazards (v1.2)

StoryA wordless story plays as animation: an opening scene, one scene after each world, and an ending. Enter, Space, Esc or a tap skips a scene.
Grab and pullHold Shift or G beside a crate and walk away to drag it. Near a hanging chain, hold G and walk away to pull it all the way and open its gate.
Gold orbsA ward orb absorbs one hit from spikes, saws or traps (up to three). A star makes you immune to them for 8 seconds. Pits and the chasing shadow still kill.
White glow = dangerAnything that can take a life pulses bright white: spikes, saws, trap spikes and hands, pits and the chasing shadow. Helpful things glow gold.

Pause menu

P / EscPause. Film grain and VHS (scanlines, colour bleed, tracking band, tape wobble, PLAY counter) are off by default. Turn them on here; the choice is remembered on this device.
